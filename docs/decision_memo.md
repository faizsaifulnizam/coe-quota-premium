# Decision memo — Singapore COE: tighter quota, or more bids?

**The answer: for cars, more bids than quota — one clean supply case, one recent puzzle.** For Categories A/B (not goods vehicles or motorcycles), exercise-to-exercise premium moves are moderately associated with **bids-per-quota** moves (Spearman ρ **+0.37** Cat A / **+0.56** Cat B since May 2022) and only weakly with **quota** moves (ρ **−0.14** / **−0.09**); Cat C/D do not follow the split — post-2022, quota is their larger correlate (ρ **−0.16 / −0.28** vs bids-per-quota **+0.04 / −0.06**). At the top-5 increases per category (25 jumps), quotas moved down 13 / up 11 / flat 1 (median **−0.1%**) while bids-per-quota rose in **17 of 25** — including the largest single increase of the whole series and Cat A's largest, both in **2024-01 R2**: Cat B **+26,990** (quota +3.8%, bids per quota 1.34 → 1.99) and Cat A **+16,579** (quota −0.1%, 1.28 → 2.04). The only large quota cut in the set: Cat C **2023-02 (+8,010)** after **−43.6%**.

**But the 2024–26 high levels are not a bid flood.** Sep 2026: Cat A's record **133,009** (latest 131,890; quota 1,195 → 1,193), Cat B **135,001** on quotas 934/927 — below its own record **150,001** (2023-10, quota 472). Cat A's average quota: **549 (2022) → 1,252 (2026)** — that's **+128%**; the **+28%** is **2024 (980) → 2026**, not from the 2022 trough. Count pressure eased into 2026 (Cat A bids-per-quota 1.58 (2024) → 1.53; success rate 64.0% → 66.1%). The 2022–23 doubling, by contrast, coincided with the squeeze (Cat B average quota 483 in 2023 vs 820 in 2021). Counts alone do not explain the last two years: the premium is set by the marginal successful **bid value**, which this file does not contain.

**Scope & definitions.** LTA COE bidding results via data.gov.sg — one row per category per exercise, 2010-01 → 2026-09; **premium = the quota premium (the lowest successful bid price for the exercise), not the PQP** (the renewal price existing owners pay). Pressure is count-based: `bids per quota = bids received ÷ quota`, `success rate = successful bids ÷ bids received`.

**The May-2022 redefinition, handled.** Category A/B rules changed at the May 2022 1st exercise (observable: Cat A quota +15.0% at the boundary, 532 → 612). The two regimes are never averaged together; the single boundary-crossing change is excluded from statistics and counted (1,975 deltas − 7 excluded = 1,968); every figure carries the break line. Categories C/D/E were not redefined; their split by the same date is a period comparison only.

**What a reader should NOT conclude.** This file cannot say *why* bidders show up, who they are, or what they bid — only how many bids met how many certificates and the price that cleared. It cannot time the market, forecast a premium, or attribute causes; it is descriptive. One source anomaly (Cat D, 2010-01 R2: S$20,090 reverting to S$852 next exercise) stays visible in the data and is noted, not trimmed.
