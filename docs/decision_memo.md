# Decision memo — Singapore COE: tighter quota, or more bids?

**Answer:** For A/B after May 2022, premium changes have a stronger descriptive association with bids-per-quota changes (**ρ +0.37 / +0.55**) than quota changes (**−0.14 / −0.09**). C/D have the opposite ordering in the base Spearman results: quota **−0.17 / −0.28**, bids-per-quota **+0.04 / −0.06**. This is co-movement, not an estimate of causes.

**Selected jumps tell a mixed story.** At the top 5 positive S$ changes per category (25 events, not the overall 25 largest), quotas moved down 13 / up 11 / flat 1, median **−0.1%**; bids-per-quota rose in **17/25**. The series’ largest increase, B **+S$26,990**, and A’s largest, **+S$16,579**, both occurred in **2024-01 R2** with quota changes **+3.8% / −0.1%** and bids-per-quota **1.34 → 1.99 / 1.28 → 2.04**. Two jumps accompanied quota cuts above 40%: **C, 2023-02 R1, +S$8,010 after −43.6%**; **D, 2020-08 R1, +S$1,191 after −42.1%**. Neither establishes a causal supply effect.

**A record does not require a falling quota.** A’s record was **S$133,009**, 2026-09 R1, quota 1,195; R2 was S$131,890, quota 1,193. For **January–September in both years**, A’s average quota rose **962 in 2024 → 1,252 in 2026 (+30%)**, while average premium rose **S$88,078 → S$119,812**. B’s September prices, S$135,001 / S$133,000, were below its October 2023 R2 record of S$150,001. The file lacks bidder reserve values, so it cannot explain why these prices cleared.

**Scope and definitions.** The snapshot has 396 exercises × 5 categories, January 2010–September 2026. Premium is the common final quota premium in S$ paid by all successful bidders in that category, not the lowest winning reserve price or the PQP. Bids-per-quota is bids submitted per certificate, **not unique participants**. Quota is announced before bidding; bids do not mechanically increase it ([LTA rules](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/upfront-vehicle-costs/certificate-of-entitlement--coe-.html)).

**Category definitions are not constant.** Taxis left A in August 2012; the power criterion began in February 2014; EV limits changed in May 2022 ([LTA historical table](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/ltagov/who_we_are/statistics_and_publications/statistics/pdf/COE_Result_2010_2014.pdf)). Earlier A/B association estimates therefore use **February 2014 R2–April 2022**, later estimates **May 2022 R2 onward**. Both endpoints use the same definition. Earlier A/B n=190 each; quota ρ −0.049 / −0.078, bids-per-quota +0.552 / +0.483. Full history remains context. Reconciliation: **1,975 − 5 pause − 2 May-boundary − 196 earlier-definition = 1,772** base changes. C/D/E’s May split is a period comparison only.

**Source accuracy is a separate question.** Two cells conflict with LTA’s historical table: 2010-02 R1 B quota **1,154 vs 693**; 2010-01 R2 D premium **S$20,090 vs S$852**. Raw values remain unchanged. Sensitivity removes deltas touching the disputed observations, including their next changes; B is already outside A/B scope, and D loses 2 more observations. D pre quota ρ changes −0.123 → −0.134 and bids-per-quota +0.069 → +0.062; post-2022 is unaffected. Structural checks do not certify source truth.

**What not to conclude:** no forecasts, causal supply/demand split, unique-bidder counts, or dealer/consumer motive claims. The ratio includes quota in its denominator; conditioning on bid-count growth is descriptive, not causal identification. Other auction-rule changes, including motorcycle TCOE/deposit changes, can also affect period comparisons.
